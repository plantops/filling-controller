## Photo notes

When adding an image that materially affects wiring or commissioning, add a nearby note or update the as-built record with:

```text
date
machine/spout
view/location
device/terminal identification
relevant measurement or observation
before/after status
```

Do not treat a photograph as proof of a safe electrical state by itself; measurements and traced wiring remain authoritative.
